# Sun and Moon

[[← Air quality|EN-Air-quality-and-pollen]] · [[Troubleshooting →|EN-Troubleshooting]]

MeteoMatrix includes animated astronomical screens inherited from the verified **SieradzMeteo v1.6** source.

## Sun

- Local **sunrise** and **sunset**.
- Animated pixel-art rise/set scenes, without directional arrow overlays.

## Moon

- Local **moonrise** and **moonset**.
- Current **lunar phase**.
- Original moon imagery and animations.

Event times depend on your configured coordinates and timezone. Check [[Configuration|EN-Configuration]] after changing location.
