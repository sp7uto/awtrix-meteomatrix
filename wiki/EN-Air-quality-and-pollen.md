# Air quality and pollen

[[← Weather|EN-Weather]] · [[Sun and Moon →|EN-Sun-and-Moon]]

MeteoMatrix includes European air quality indicators and seasonal plant pollen data where available.

- **European AQI:** a summary air quality index; higher means poorer air quality.
- **PM2.5:** fine particulate matter concentration.
- **PM10:** particulate matter concentration.
- **Pollen:** seasonally available plant allergen data.

Open-Meteo information may be model-based rather than direct measurements at your exact address. Data coverage, especially pollen, depends on region and season. An unavailable value does not necessarily mean zero.
