# Introducing MeteoMatrix

[[← Home|Home]] · [[Installation →|EN-Installation]]

**MeteoMatrix** is a weather and environment dashboard for **Ulanzi TC001 (32 × 8 pixels)** running **AWTRIX NG**.

Its **26 screens** cover local weather, European air quality, seasonal pollen and Sun/Moon events. The project preserves original 8×8 pixel-art graphics and animations from the verified SieradzMeteo v1.6.

## Highlights

- Local weather, feels-like temperature, rain, snow and wind
- Pressure, cloud cover and air quality
- Sunrise/sunset, moonrise/moonset and lunar phase
- Coordinates, IANA timezone and Polish/English labels
- Open-Meteo integration
